💡 How to Use:

  • Click "Start Camera" to begin
  • Pinch thumb + index finger together = pen down
  • Move your pinched hand in the air to write
  • Open your hand = pen up (move without drawing)
  • Make a fist = clear the whole canvas
  • "Change Color" cycles through pen colors
  • "Undo" removes your last stroke
  • Good lighting helps hand detection
  • Your camera data stays on your device
